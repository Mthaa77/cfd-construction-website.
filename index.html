Welcome to CFD

Good work. Done properly.

Founded by Charl Allen, a builder by trade who began learning the craft at seventeen, CFD Construction brings hands-on experience to home renovations, maintenance and custom work. The team takes on one project at a time, with work supervised from start to finish and attention given to the details that shape the final result.

Every home and job has its own requirements. CFD discusses the scope, suitable materials and timing with you before work begins. From repairs and surface finishes to custom welding and carpentry, the aim is a practical result and a straightforward working relationship. References are available on request.

Good questions

Before you ask.

Straight answers to common questions about working with CFD.

How do I get a quote? Step 01

CFD's contact page invites quote bookings via WhatsApp, telephone or email — pick whichever suits you, or use the form on this page. CFD will discuss the scope and materials with you before work begins. Ask for the quote and timing that fit your project.

Do you take on small repair jobs? Step 02

Yes — "there is no job too small" is CFD's own line, and it's the backbone of their maintenance service. The interactive snag-list builder can help you organise several small jobs into one clear message.

Do you offer custom steelwork and carpentry? Step 03

Yes. CFD’s published service list includes customised welding, steelwork, carpentry and woodwork. Contact the team to discuss the design, measurements and scope of your project.

Which areas do you serve? Step 04

CFD is based in Edenvale, Gauteng. Send your suburb and a short description of the work when you get in touch to discuss availability.

Do you clean up after the work? Step 05

Yes — CFD's own site commits to cleaning up after themselves rather than leaving properties messy, backed by strong after-sales service. It's one of the details customers notice first and mention unprompted.

Can I see references? Step 06

References are available on request. The project gallery features examples published in CFD’s existing portfolio.
